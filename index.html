Naamrit

Naam jap, chanting and scripture for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Naamrit is a calm, offline app for Hindu naam jap. Count on a 108-bead mala with gentle haptics. You can count from the Lock Screen, your earphones, your Apple Watch, or with Pocket Mala (voice, a knock on your pocket, or a nod with AirPods) while the phone stays away. Chant over a tanpura or your own recorded chant, and read the Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana, Upanishads and more as a book with public-domain paintings. Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.
