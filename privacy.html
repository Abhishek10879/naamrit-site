Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

In short: Naamrit does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We, the developers, cannot see your counts, recordings, health data or anything else you do in the app.

This policy covers the Naamrit app for iPhone and Apple Watch, including its widgets, Lock Screen counter and Control Center control.

1. What stays on your device

Naamrit saves the following on your device so the app works. None of it is sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your jap: the current count and mala, your daily goal, your daily history of jap, and your settings. They are kept in one small file in the app's private storage, which is shared only with Naamrit's own widgets.
  • Preferences: your theme, reading position in books, Pocket Mala choices, any custom naam or mantra you add, and the start and end time of your last sitting.
  • Calibration: when you set up voice, knock or nod counting, the app saves a few numbers such as a loudness level or a sensitivity. It does not save audio or motion recordings.
  • Your recorded chant, if you choose to make one (up to 30 seconds). It stays on your iPhone until you delete it.
  • On Apple Watch: a copy of your current count and naam, so the Watch can show it.

2. Network use

Naamrit does not connect to any server of ours, because we don't run one. The app does not go online by itself. All scripture, paintings, sounds and guidance are built into the app. Your device only uses the internet when you use one of Apple's services or tap a link:

  • In-app purchases (optional Dakshina) are handled by Apple's App Store. See section 5.
  • iPhone and Apple Watch sync uses Apple's WatchConnectivity to send your count between your own paired iPhone and Watch.
  • Links you tap open outside the app. For example, a helpline's website or a painting's source page opens in Safari, and a helpline number opens the Phone app. Sharing a verse opens the iOS Share sheet, and you choose where it goes.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: if you use Siri to count, Apple handles the voice request under Apple's own privacy policy. Naamrit only receives the "count one jap" action.

3. Permissions and why we ask

Naamrit asks for a permission only when you turn on the feature that needs it. You can change any of them at any time in Settings → Naamrit on your iPhone.

Microphone

  • Voice counting (also used in Pocket Mala) listens to how loud your chanting is, so it can tell when each jap ends. It measures loudness only. It does not record, store, recognise or send your voice or words.
  • Record your own chant: if you choose to, you can record yourself chanting so it loops over the tanpura. The recording is saved only on your iPhone. You can delete it in the chant player.

While the microphone is in use, iPhone shows its orange microphone indicator.

Motion and fitness

  • Knock counting uses the iPhone's motion sensor to notice two quick taps on your pocket.
  • Nod counting uses head motion from supported AirPods to notice a small nod.
  • Motion activity (whether you seem to be walking or travelling) is used only to adjust knock and nod counting, so bumps on the road are not counted.

Motion data is processed on your device the moment it arrives. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Apple Health is optional and off until you tap Connect Apple Health. If you connect it:

  • Naamrit writes each jap sitting to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes.
  • Naamrit reads Mindful Minutes (to show today's total), plus heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability recorded by your Apple Watch around your last sitting. This is only to show them to you on the Sadhana screen.

Health data read by Naamrit:

  • is shown only to you, inside the app, and is not saved by the app;
  • never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • is never used for advertising or marketing, or sold, or shared with third parties;
  • is not stored in iCloud by Naamrit.

The heart readings are your own measurements and are not medical advice. You can stop Naamrit's access at any time in the Health app → your profile → Apps (or Privacy → Apps) → Naamrit, or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

5. In-app purchases (Dakshina)

Dakshina is an optional tip. It does not unlock anything, and everything in Naamrit is free to use. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and Naamrit never sees your name, Apple Account, or payment details. The app only remembers on your device how many times you have given, so it can say thank you. Apple handles purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. "What's troubling you?"

When you type what is troubling you, the app matches your words to Gita verses on your device. Your text is not saved and is not sent anywhere. It is gone when you leave the screen. If your words suggest self-harm, the app shows helpline numbers. Calling or visiting one is up to you.

7. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

8. Children

Naamrit is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your data and how to delete it

Your data is on your devices, so you control it:

  • Delete your recorded chant: open the chant player in Naamrit and tap the delete button next to your recording.
  • Delete everything in the app: delete Naamrit from your iPhone (and Apple Watch). This removes your counts, history, settings and recording.
  • Apple Health: deleting the app does not remove Mindful Minutes it already saved to Health. You can delete them, and turn off Naamrit's access, in the Health app.
  • Backups: if you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple includes the app's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. Apple manages those backups, not us.

10. Changes to this policy

If Naamrit ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If an update collects any new kind of data, we will say so clearly here and in the App Store listing.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/Abhishek10879/naamrit-site/issues. Issues are public, so please don't include personal or health details.
